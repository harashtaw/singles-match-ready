Court Geometry & Sprint Distance Reduction Matrix

Visual Cues of Disguised Drop Shots

1. Racquet Deceleration: Swing speed accelerates initially, then suddenly slows right before contact.

2. Open Racquet Face & Chest: Shoulder rotation halts; racquet face opens flat or upward.

3. Off-Hand Drop: Non-dominant hand drops off racquet throat early to establish slice posture.

Biomechanical Stroke Cues

2HB Long Error Fix: Lead with non-dominant left hand up & through; keep chin quiet on front shoulder through follow-through.

Shoulder-Safe 2nd Serve: Toss at 12:30/1:00 o'clock and brush 7 to 1 o'clock with wrist snap instead of arching behind head.
